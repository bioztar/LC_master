# Refunds Policy

## Eligibility
Refunds available within 30 days of purchase for unused items in original packaging.
Digital goods: refundable within 14 days if not downloaded more than 3 times.
Subscriptions: prorated refund for unused period if cancelled within 7 days of renewal.

## Process
1. Customer submits request via support portal or email support@example.com.
2. Agent verifies order in OMS using order ID (format: ORD-XXXXXX).
3. Approved refunds processed to original payment method within 5-7 business days.
4. Refunds over $500 require supervisor approval.

## Exclusions
- Final sale items (marked with FS- prefix in SKU).
- Gift cards (non-refundable).
- Custom or personalized items.

## Common Issues
- "Refund not received": check pending status in payment processor; can take up to 10 business days for credit cards.
- "Partial refund only": shipping fees non-refundable unless item arrived damaged.
