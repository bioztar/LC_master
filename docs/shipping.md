# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times
- Standard (US): 5-7 business days, free over $50.
- Express (US): 2-3 business days, $15 flat.
- Overnight (US): next business day if ordered before 2pm ET, $30 flat.
- International: 10-21 business days, calculated at checkout.

## Tracking
Tracking number emailed within 24h of dispatch. Format: 1Z... (UPS), 9400... (USPS).
Customers can self-serve at /track using order ID.

## Lost or Delayed Packages
- Domestic: open investigation after 10 business days past expected delivery.
- International: 30 business days.
- Lost confirmed: free replacement or full refund, customer choice.

## Address Changes
- Before dispatch: change via account portal up to 1 hour after order.
- After dispatch: contact carrier directly; we cannot modify in-flight shipments.

## Damaged Packages
Customer must report within 7 days with photos. Free replacement, no return required for items under $100.
