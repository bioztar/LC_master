# Account & Billing

## Password Reset
Self-serve at /reset. Email link expires in 30 minutes. If no email arrives within 5 minutes, check spam, then contact support.

## Account Lockout
After 5 failed login attempts, account locks for 15 minutes. Persistent lockouts may indicate compromise — agent must verify identity via security questions + email confirmation before manual unlock.

## Billing Disputes
- Unrecognized charge: provide last 4 of card + date; agent looks up in billing system.
- Duplicate charge: usually a pending auth that drops in 3-5 days; if persists past 7 days, escalate to billing team.
- Subscription cancelled but charged: confirm cancellation date vs renewal date; refund if cancelled before renewal.

## Plan Changes
- Upgrades: immediate, prorated charge.
- Downgrades: effective next billing cycle, no refund for current cycle.
- Plan cancellation: access retained until end of paid period.

## Data Export & Deletion (GDPR/CCPA)
- Export: self-serve in account settings, ZIP delivered within 24h.
- Deletion request: 30-day grace period, then irreversible. Requires email + identity verification. Escalate to privacy@example.com for processing.
